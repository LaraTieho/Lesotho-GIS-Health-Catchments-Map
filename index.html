User guide

This map shows health facility catchments, villages, schools, roads, and settlement extents for all ten districts of Lesotho. Use it for EPI outreach planning, microplanning, and service access analysis.

Moving around the map

Click and drag to pan the map. Use the + and − buttons at the top left to zoom in and out. Your scroll wheel or trackpad also zooms.

To return to the full extent of Lesotho, refresh the page.

Switching layers on and off

Click the layers icon in the top-right corner to expand the layers panel. Tick or untick the checkbox next to a layer name to show or hide it on the map. Layers are grouped by district; scroll down the panel to find other districts.

Turning off road or village layers when viewing a large area makes the map easier to read.

Reading feature details

Click any feature — a catchment boundary, village dot, road line, or health facility marker — to open a popup showing its recorded attributes such as population, facility name, or road type.

Close the popup by clicking × or clicking elsewhere on the map.

Identifying features by colour

Village dots are shaded from white to red based on travel distance to the nearest health facility. White dots are within 4.5 km; red dots are over 15 km away. The legend in the layers panel shows the full range for each district.

Searching for a location

Use the search box at the top left of the map to find Villages, Health Facilities, and Districts in this map database. Type at least two characters and a list of matching results will appear, each labelled with its type. Click a result to fly the map to that location.

About this dataset

The i button at the bottom left shows a full description of the dataset: its sources, validation process, and intended use. Hover over it to expand the text.

This dataset is for operational planning and analysis. It is not a legal boundary dataset. Always use it alongside the latest approved version and change log.
